Trophies for Schools in Delhi

School awards carry a weight out of proportion to their size — a child keeps a school trophy for years. Shreeji Corporates makes trophies, medals and awards for schools in New Delhi: annual day prizes, sports day medals, academic awards and farewells, customised with your school’s name and logo and engraved with each student’s name. There is no minimum order.

Annual day, sports day and everything between

A school year has a rhythm of occasions, and each one needs its own kind of award. Annual day calls for the formal prizes — academic excellence, all-round achievement, the named trophies a school gives every year. Sports day calls for medals and cups in numbers, across every event and age group. Farewells call for mementos that carry a personal line. Inter-house competitions run through the year and need a consistent set that can be added to term after term.

Because the same school returns for each occasion, the designs are kept consistent — the school name and logo set out the same way every time, so this year's prizes belong with last year's.

Names, classes and categories — got right

The hard part of a school order is not the trophies; it is the list. Dozens of names, classes and categories, and every single one has to be spelled right — a misspelled name on a child's trophy is the one thing nobody forgets. The engraving layout for every piece is set out for your approval before anything is made, so the school checks the names once, on paper, rather than discovering an error on the day.

Send the list in whatever form the school keeps it — a spreadsheet, a document, a message. We will set it out piece by piece and send it back for checking before production.

From one prize to the whole prize day

There is no minimum order. A single farewell memento gets the same care as the full sports day set, because to the student receiving it, it is the whole occasion. Schools order in every size: one cup for a house championship, a class set of medals, or the complete annual day production with every category covered.

For the big occasions, start as soon as the date is fixed. The categories and the design can be settled early; the final names list usually comes last, and the timeline is planned around it.

How ordering works

There is no checkout and no form to fill in. Ordering is a conversation, and it takes three steps.

1. Tell us on WhatsApp or email

Send the occasion, the design idea and the quantity — a message on WhatsApp to +91 92669 44393, or an email to shreejicorporates@gmail.com. A photo of a trophy you like, a logo, or a rough sketch all help. If you are not sure what you want yet, say so; that is a normal starting point.

2. We design it with you

We suggest shapes, materials and sizes for the occasion and the budget you have in mind, and work out the engraving — the names, the wording, a logo or a motif. You approve the design before anything is made. Nothing goes into production until you are happy with it.

3. Made and delivered

Consultation, design, production and delivery happen under one roof. Every piece is checked before it leaves, and packed to arrive unbroken. Engraving is included, and every order is made to order — there is no warehouse of ready-made trophies with someone else's name on them.

Because everything is made to order, the right time to get in touch is as soon as you know the date of the event. A clear brief and an approved design early on is what makes a smooth delivery.
