Crystal is the most formal material a trophy can be made from — heavy, clear and unmistakable on a desk or a stage. Why crystal, and when it is the right choice

Crystal suits the occasions that need weight — literally and figuratively. A lifetime achievement award, a retirement, a company's highest honour, a milestone anniversary: these are the moments where a crystal trophy says what a lighter material cannot. It is the material people keep on their desk for years, and it is the one that photographs best on a stage.

That does not make it right for everything. For a school sports day with dozens of winners, crystal would be heavy and costly; acrylic or metal usually fits better. For a single senior recognition, or a small set of top awards at a corporate night, crystal is the natural choice. Part of the design conversation is exactly this judgement — matching the material to the moment, not just to a budget.

The crystal range

Crystal trophies come in several forms, and the shape should follow the award. A tall tower is a clear, upright statement — it reads well from a distance and stands out in a photograph. A globe carries a different meaning: it suits global achievement, international programmes and anything where the world is the point. A faceted column crowned with a gold star is the most ceremonial of the three, and it is the piece we recommend when the award is the single highest honour of the evening.

Within those forms, the customisation is in the engraving and the proportions. The height, the base, the wording and the logo are all tailored. Crystal takes laser engraving beautifully — the text sits in the surface with a clean, frosted edge, and a logo engraves with the same precision. The engraving is included with every piece, and you approve the layout before it is cut.

Engraving crystal: what to send us

For the best result, send the exact wording as you want it to appear — the recipient's name, the award title, the date — and the logo as a clear file if you want it engraved. If you have a previous trophy whose wording you liked, a photo of it helps. We will set out the engraving, show you the layout, and only proceed once you have approved it.

Crystal is unforgiving of mistakes, which is why the approval step matters. Once the laser has cut, the engraving cannot be undone. That is also why every piece is checked before it leaves the studio, and packed to arrive unbroken — crystal is heavy and it needs proper packing, not a thin box.
