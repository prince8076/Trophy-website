What makes a corporate gift work

A corporate gift fails when it feels generic — the same item, the same branding, given to everyone, remembered by no one. It works when it feels chosen: the right piece, for the right person, with the company's branding carried properly. That is the difference between a gift and a handout, and it is the whole point of customising.

The best corporate gifts are the ones that stay visible. A crystal piece on a desk is seen every day; a well-made memento on a shelf is seen by visitors. That visibility is what the company is paying for — not the object itself, but the fact that it is still there, months later, carrying the brand. A cheap gift that is thrown away is not a saving; it is a message that the relationship was not worth much.

Gifts for clients, partners and employees

Different recipients call for different gifts. A client or a partner deserves the most considered piece — crystal, engraved with care, and chosen for the relationship. An employee gift can be warmer and more personal, and it often carries a message about the year or the milestone. A dealer or a distributor gift sits between the two: it should carry the brand clearly, and it should be consistent across the whole set, so every dealer receives the same standard.

For festive gifting and year-end programmes, the numbers are usually the largest, and the timeline is the tightest. Get in touch as soon as you know the approximate numbers — the design and the material can be settled early, and the names or the branding list can follow.

Branding, packing and delivery

Your logo is laser-engraved into the gift, not stuck on it, and the layout is approved by you before production. Every gift is checked before it leaves and packed to arrive unbroken — securely packed matters more for gifts than for anything else, because a gift that arrives damaged is worse than no gift at all.

Delivery is handled under the same roof as production, so the packing is done by the people who made the gift. If the gifts are going to multiple addresses, tell us early — the packing and the list can be worked out together rather than at the last minute.
