Acrylic Trophies & Awards

Acrylic is the most flexible trophy material — light, modern, and able to take almost any shape, size or colour. Shreeji Corporates makes customised acrylic trophies, plaques and awards in New Delhi, with your branding engraved or printed, for schools, sports events, corporate programmes and ceremonies. Bulk orders are welcome, and every piece is made to order.

Why acrylic works for so many occasions

Acrylic's strength is that it does not impose a shape. Crystal wants to be a tower or a globe; metal wants to be a cup. Acrylic can be a star, a plaque, a shield, a freeform shape cut to your logo, or a layered design in two or three colours. That makes it the right material when the award itself should carry the theme — a star for a rising star award, a shape that matches an event's branding, a plaque that sits flat on a desk.

It is also light. A school ordering fifty trophies for a sports day, or a company ordering a set for a dealer meet, will notice the difference in handling and in delivery. And because acrylic takes colour well, it is the material to choose when the award should match a brand colour or an event theme rather than stay in clear crystal or silver metal.

Shapes, sizes and colours

There is no fixed catalogue of acrylic shapes. The size, the shape and the colour are tailored to the order — a small star for a participation award, a tall plaque for a winner, a thick freeform piece for a special recognition. Your branding can be engraved or printed, and the engraving is included with every piece.

When you get in touch, the most useful details are the occasion, the number of pieces, and whether you have a shape or a colour in mind. A sketch, a logo, or a photo of something you liked are all useful starting points. We will suggest what works in acrylic for that brief, and you approve the design before anything is cut.

Acrylic for bulk orders

Acrylic is the material most often chosen for bulk orders, because it is consistent piece to piece and it scales well. A school sports day, a tournament with many categories, a corporate programme with an award for every region — these are the orders where acrylic's lightness and flexibility pay off. The engraving stays precise across the whole run, because each piece is engraved to the same approved layout.

For bulk orders, the timeline matters more than for a single piece. Get in touch as soon as you know the date and the approximate numbers, even if the final names are not ready yet — the design and the material can be settled early, and the engraving list can follow.

Frequently asked questions

Can acrylic trophies be made in any shape?

Yes. Acrylic can be cut to almost any shape, size or colour — a star, a plaque, a shield, a freeform shape or a design matched to your logo. Tell us the shape or theme you have in mind and we will work out the design with you.

Can you print or engrave our branding on acrylic?

Yes. Your branding can be engraved or printed on acrylic trophies, and engraving is included with every piece. Send your logo and the wording you want, and we will set out the layout for your approval.

Are acrylic trophies good for bulk orders?

Yes. Acrylic is light, consistent piece to piece and scales well, which makes it the usual choice for school sports days, tournaments with many categories and corporate programmes. Contact us early with the date and approximate numbers.

What is the difference between acrylic and crystal trophies?

Crystal is heavier and more formal, and suits senior recognition and milestones. Acrylic is lighter, more flexible in shape and colour, and suits larger numbers and themed awards. We will help you pick the right material for your occasion.
