How ordering works

There is no checkout and no form to fill in. Ordering is a conversation, and it takes three steps.

1. Tell us on WhatsApp or email

Send the occasion, the design idea and the quantity — a message on WhatsApp to +91 92669 44393, or an email to shreejicorporates@gmail.com. A photo of a trophy you like, a logo, or a rough sketch all help. If you are not sure what you want yet, say so; that is a normal starting point.

2. We design it with you

We suggest shapes, materials and sizes for the occasion and the budget you have in mind, and work out the engraving — the names, the wording, a logo or a motif. You approve the design before anything is made. Nothing goes into production until you are happy with it.

3. Made and delivered

Consultation, design, production and delivery happen under one roof. Every piece is checked before it leaves, and packed to arrive unbroken. Engraving is included, and every order is made to order — there is no warehouse of ready-made trophies with someone else's name on them.

Because everything is made to order, the right time to get in touch is as soon as you know the date of the event. A clear brief and an approved design early on is what makes a smooth delivery.
