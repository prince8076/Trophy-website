Medals for tournaments, schools and events

A medal order is usually a bulk order, and bulk is where consistency matters most. Fifty medals for a school sports day, two hundred for a tournament, a full set for every age group — each one has to look like the others, with the same event name, the same logo and the same finish. That consistency is the difference between a medal that feels earned and one that feels mass-produced.

Custom medals are made to your design: the event name, the category, the year, a logo or a motif. The design is approved by you before production, so what arrives is the medal you signed off on, not a surprise. Ribbons, where they are part of the order, are matched to the event as well.

Medals and trophies together

Most tournaments order medals and trophies together, and they should be planned together. The winner's cup sits at the top, the medals cover the field, and the engraving style runs across both so the whole set reads as one event. Planning them together also means the timeline is worked out once, not twice — which matters when the final is a fixed date and the medals have to be ready for the presentation.

For a tournament, send the name of the event, the categories, the number of medals in each category, and the date of the final. The design can be settled early; the exact split across categories can be confirmed closer to the day.

What to get right when ordering medals

Three things decide whether a medal order goes smoothly. First, the design — the event name and logo need to be clear files, not a blurry photo, so they reproduce cleanly. Second, the numbers — an honest count per category, with a few spares, because a missing medal on the day is worse than a spare in a drawer. Third, the timeline — medals are made to order, so the earlier the design is approved, the smoother the delivery. Get in touch as soon as the date is fixed, even if the final numbers are not.
